Wavy Suite for Windows (64-bit)
===============================

Run WavySuite-<version>-Windows-Setup.exe. It installs every Wavy plugin:
  - VST3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\Wavy Suite
  - CLAP to C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP\Wavy Suite
Pick "Choose plugins and formats" to leave some out, then rescan plugins in your DAW.
Uninstall from Settings > Apps > Wavy Suite.

The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn: click "More info", then "Run anyway".
To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt on the Releases page:
  certutil -hashfile WavySuite-<version>-Windows-Setup.exe SHA256
